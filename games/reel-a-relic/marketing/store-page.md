# Store Page — paste-ready copy & asset guide

Same rules as the root game's [`marketing/store-page.md`](../../marketing/store-page.md): honest
metadata that matches gameplay (mismatches get suppressed), the genre keyword in the title, no
Robux-bait phrasing, daily-return hooks up front.

One difference worth naming. The geode game competes on *collection*. This one competes on a
**verb** — you fight the fish in — and fishing is a crowded, proven category on Roblox, which
cuts both ways: the audience already exists and already knows what a fishing game is, so the
copy's whole job is to say **what this one does that a click-to-catch game doesn't**. Lead with
the fight everywhere: title-adjacent, first line, first thumbnail.

## Name (exact)

```
Reel a Relic! [Fishing Simulator]
```

`[Fishing Simulator]` is the searchable keyword and it is what people type. Keep the brackets.

## Short hook (first line of the description — shows in search previews)

```
🎣 FIGHT every fish in! 33 real species + 11 sunken relics — and your aquarium earns coins WHILE YOU SLEEP! 🐠
```

Three hooks, in the order they matter: **the fight** (the differentiator), **the number** (an
unfinished set is why someone comes back tomorrow), **while you sleep** (what makes a lapsed
player reopen the app a week later).

## Genre & subgenre (the settings dropdown)

- **Genre: `Simulation`**
- **Subgenre: `Incremental Simulator`**

Roblox's own definition of Incremental Simulator is "progression involves simple repetitive
actions to increase a counter, and as players progress, they often unlock new capabilities,
levels, and characters" — that is this game exactly: cast, catch, sell, buy a better rod, unlock
the next ocean. It is also where the audience that already plays fishing games browses, so it is
both the honest answer and the profitable one.

**Same pair as Crack a Geode, deliberately.** They are the same engine with different verbs, and
sitting in one subgenre means a player who bounces off one can be recommended the other. That is
the portfolio working as a portfolio.

Do **not** pick `Idle` — Roblox defines it as "little to no player input", and the reel-in is the
whole product. Claiming Idle would misdescribe the game to the algorithm and to the player, and
metadata that mismatches gameplay gets suppressed. The Simulation subgenres are Idle, Incremental
Simulator, Physics Sim, Sandbox, Tycoon and Vehicle Sim — there is no fishing-specific subgenre,
so `[Fishing Simulator]` in the **title** is what carries the theme. That is why the bracket
keyword matters more here than it does for the geode game.

## Icon (512x512)

A single hooked fish breaking the surface with the line taut and spray coming off it — motion,
not a portrait. Shoot it in Studio: catch something big in the Twilight Reef, pause on the splash,
get the camera low and close to the waterline so the fish fills the frame against the sunset
water. Crop square, bump saturation.

Text on the icon: none, or one word (`REEL IT IN!`). Icons with less text read better at the size
they are actually seen. If you want a second option to A/B, use Poseidon's Trident coming out of
an open chest in a shaft of light — but test it *against* the fish, don't assume; the fish shows
the verb and the trident only shows a prize.

## Thumbnails (1920x1080, make 3)

1. **The fight** — the reel bar mid-catch, meter nearly full, fish hard against the top of the
   bracket, text: `DON'T LOSE IT!` This is the best thumbnail the game has, because it shows a
   **verb**. A stranger scrolling past learns in half a second what they would actually be doing,
   which no amount of pretty water does. Make this one first and make it thumbnail #1.
2. **The Moonlit reveal** — the reveal card mid-burst on a Moonlit fish with `1 IN 500?!` on it.
   The rarity fantasy, borrowed straight from the geode game where it already works.
3. **Offline earnings** — the welcome-back popup showing `+18,400 coins while you were away`,
   with the full aquarium visible behind it, text: `IT EARNS WHILE YOU SLEEP`.

A fourth if you have time: **the Abyss reveal** — sailing in for the first time, water gone
black, Megalodon on the line. Sell the last ocean to someone standing in the first one.

Rules: real screenshots only, readable at small size, one focal point each. Swap #2 for your
actual rarest community catch once players start posting them — a real player's pull outperforms
anything you can stage, because it is proof the rare thing is reachable.

## Settings checklist

- Genre: **Simulation** · Subgenre: **Incremental Simulator**
- Devices: **Computer + Phone + Tablet** — the reel-in is one hold input, deliberately, so it
  plays identically with a finger and a mouse. Most simulator revenue is mobile; do not skip this.
- Max players: **20** (5 areas x 12 claim-exclusive buoys = 60 spots, so contention is not the
  limit here — server liveliness is; a busy dock sells the game to a new player)
- Private servers: **99 Robux/month** (set once, never reprice)
- Age questionnaire: answer **yes** to paid random items — chests are random-content items bought
  with an earnable currency. The game already shows exact odds and policy-gates restricted
  players, so answering yes costs nothing and answering no is a compliance problem.
- Enable **Analytics** and check the onboarding funnel weekly
