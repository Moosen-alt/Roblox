# Verified external source options

The ZIP includes original, import-ready OBJ stand-ins for every current Reel a Relic fish/relic
and every current Crack a Geode base crystal. You can start uploading immediately without
depending on a marketplace login.

For higher-detail replacement art, these are the safest sources I verified.

## Quaternius Animated Cute Fish Pack
- 52 animated models, plus fishing rods and lures
- FBX, OBJ and Blend
- CC0
- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/cutefish.html
- Official download folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QK5NCgIxUZ1cYGzVwaNfcfEiQhvczqUB?usp=sharing

## Quaternius Animated Fish Pack
- 7 aquatic species
- FBX, OBJ and Blend
- CC0
- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedfish.html
- Official download folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1SvlOveJJjmhSn-FgCRyojc1T5QHjjGkF?usp=sharing

## Kenney Pirate Kit
- Useful for chests, bottles, swords/cutlasses, ships and pirate scenery
- CC0
- https://www.kenney.nl/

## Poly Haven
- Best fit for CC0 rocks, cave props and environmental materials
- https://polyhaven.com/models

## NASA 3D
- Public-domain space assets
- Safe, but not especially useful for these two current games
- https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov/models

## Sites to review per asset before redistribution
Freepik, Threedio, Sketchfab, CGTrader, TurboSquid, Fab, Free3D, 3DExport and GrabCAD.
A free download is not automatically a redistribution license. For a monetized Roblox game,
prefer CC0 or CC-BY and preserve any required attribution.
